TDL

Personal To-Do-List For Your Desktop

More details

TDL is a lightweight desktop to-do list app built for tracking daily tasks over time, not just a flat checklist.

It offers four ways to look at your work:
Day View: for managing today's tasks one at a time.
Week View: laid out as columns you can drag tasks between.
Month View: with an at-a-glance calendar showing how complete each day was.
Year View: that renders the whole year as a grid of days shaded by completion, similar to a habit-tracking heatmap.

Tasks can be one-off or set to repeat daily, weekly, monthly, or yearly, and the app keeps a running streak counter for consecutive fully-completed days

Everyday conveniences are built in throughout: drag-and-drop or keyboard reordering, undo and redo for any change, bulk actions like clearing completed tasks or marking a whole day done, and full export/import of your data as a JSON backup.
It also supports light and dark themes.

TDL runs as a native Windows desktop app (built with Tauri) and stores all data locally on your machine — nothing is sent to a server. It's a personal project by Frank Zito, built to be a fast, no-frills alternative to bulkier task-management software.
